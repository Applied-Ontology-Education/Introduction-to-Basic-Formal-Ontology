# Assignments

The formal course assessment centers on an essay and a presentation addressing an issue concerning BFO's use or reception. Learners should consult the [complete course page](https://aowiki.nsm.buffalo.edu/index.php/Introduction_to_Basic_Formal_Ontology:_Fall_2026) for current deadlines and instructor guidance.

## Suggested workflow

1. Select a focused question about BFO's foundations, formalization, application, comparison with another top-level ontology, or reception.
2. Identify a concrete case, ontology, or modeling problem.
3. State the relevant BFO distinctions and definitions precisely.
4. Analyze the case using primary sources and suitable examples.
5. Address a credible objection, limitation, or alternative analysis.
6. Develop the analysis into an essay and a concise presentation.
7. Revise both products in light of feedback.

## Submission checklist

- The central question is explicit.
- BFO terms are used consistently and accurately.
- Claims are supported with citations or concrete examples.
- The analysis distinguishes description from evaluation.
- The conclusion answers the central question.
- Slides are readable, focused, and appropriately attributed.
- Links and media function before submission.

The authoritative grading and submission requirements remain on the complete course page.
